Heroku we were in a beautiful relationship for so many years. Why did you leave us high and dry in this cold world? I must say it hurts. Heroku was the best option available for someone who wants to validate an idea or build an MVP quickly. With a few clicks, we could deploy a full-stack application in minutes. Now that a beautiful relationship has ended, we must move on. Below are the three best options we got today.

Render — [https://render.com](https://render.com)

Railway -[https://railway.app](https://railway.app)

Cyclic — [https://cyclic.sh](https://cyclic.sh)

They all have a free tier and are very easy to deploy. All three have options to deploy from GitHub and very fast to expose an endpoint. Give them a try, and let me know how you feel about them. Also, leave your thoughts in the comments or if you know of other options. Happy coding.
