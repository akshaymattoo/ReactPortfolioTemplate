Let's start with why. I have a brand new MacBook and a 2-year-old decided to step on it, and the screen was broken. I took it to the apple store and they charged 600$ for the new screen. I felt it was not worth it and I started using it with an external monitor. One problem I faced was Mac doesn’t allow you to keep the lid closed and also connect it to an external monitor. After doing some research I found there is an app [Amphetamine](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/amphetamine/id937984704?mt=12) that came to the rescue. This app does a lot more than keep the Mac active while the lid is closed. There are many articles as well that have described what it does.

To add on top I thought I would buy a portable monitor so whenever I am traveling I will be able to work. And there are many options available on [amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=portable+monitor&crid=2OPV62SYM0C9E&sprefix=portable+monitor%2Caps%2C140&ref=nb_sb_noss_1). I bought the one worth 119$. So I was able to save 500$ on the screen and also keep the MacBook.

Wishing everyone luck with 2-year-old toddlers, macbook’s are at risk, but we have a cheap solution.
