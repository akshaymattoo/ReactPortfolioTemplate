I am building a fitness app for busy people who want to work up a sweat without needing a large block of free time.

The project is still in progress. I am exploring how to make exercise feel practical on a crowded day, with short sessions and a simple experience that helps people begin instead of spending time planning.

I will add more here as the idea, workouts, and product take shape.
