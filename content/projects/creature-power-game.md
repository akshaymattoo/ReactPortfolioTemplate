I am building this game for my six-year-old son. It turns his interest in animals and *Wild Kratts* into a simple game we can enjoy together.

The player guesses an animal from clues. Each correct answer unlocks a collectible disc, giving every round a small sense of discovery and progress.

The idea is still growing. I want the clues to feel understandable for a six-year-old, the animals to stay interesting, and the collecting mechanic to make him want to learn one more fact before stopping.

This is also an experiment in building with AI: starting with a personal idea, making a playable version quickly, and improving it by watching how my son actually uses it.
